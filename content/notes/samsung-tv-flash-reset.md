---
title: "Samsung TV flash reset"
date: 2026-09-28
tags: [samsung, tv, hardware]
---

When a Samsung TV hangs, boot-loops or won't respond, a flash reset often
brings it back.

1. Press and hold the button on the TV itself, below the remote control
   sensor light, for **3 seconds**.
2. Release it.
3. Press and hold the **on/off** button on the remote for **5 seconds**.
4. The TV should restart several times. That is expected.

If it doesn't work, try a *soft* reset first: hold down the on/off button on
the remote until the TV restarts. Then repeat the steps above.
