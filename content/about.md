I'm a software engineer at the [Qt Group](https://www.qt.io/) in Berlin, where
I work on Qt Design Studio. Before that I built C++ backends and automotive
HMIs, and spent several years in visualization research and teaching at the
Bauhaus-Universität Weimar.

## Experience

**Staff Software Engineer**, Qt Group, Berlin · *since 01/2021*\
Implementing the QML/C++ UI and backend logic of Qt Design Studio, building
and maintaining views and components in line with our design system,
communicating problems and challenges in proposed UX solutions, and
prototyping new ideas in standalone research projects, such as a Figma-style
collaborative design tool built with Qt for WebAssembly and Firebase.

**C++ / UI Developer**, Siili Solutions GmbH, Berlin · *01/2017 – 12/2020*\
HMI development of a digital instrument cluster for a global automotive
electronics supplier, a C/C++ hardware abstraction layer, R&D for internal
Qt 3D demos, and development of Qt Design Studio as an external employee.

**C++ Developer**, Information Design One, Frankfurt am Main · *10/2016 – 12/2017*\
Development of highly available data hubs (enterprise application
integration) for the aviation industry: message translators, database
management systems and communication protocols.

**Backend Developer C++**, CeleraOne, Berlin · *04/2016 – 09/2016*\
Enhancement of CeleraOne's in-memory architecture and business logic,
efficient algorithms and data structures for real-time processing of large
data volumes, and multithreading in modern C++.

**Teaching and Research Assistant**, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, Virtual Reality and Visualization Research · *11/2012 – 12/2015*\
Development of information visualizations, supervision of student projects
and theses, a weekly lab course accompanying the visualization lecture,
participation in a research project funded by the BMBF, new approaches to
visualizing multivariate time-varying data, and scientific publications.

**Research Assistant**, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, Virtual Reality and Visualization Research · *02/2010 – 10/2012*\
Development of information visualizations and preparation of scientific
publications.

**Student Assistant**, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, Virtual Reality and Visualization Research · *04/2009 – 07/2009*\
Development of information visualizations.

## Teaching

At the Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, together with Bernd Fröhlich, Patrick
Riehmann, Martin Potthast and Sebastian Thiele:

- **Course: Visualization** (SS 13, SS 14, SS 15). Fundamental and advanced
  techniques of information and scientific visualization, with a lab course
  implementing volume rendering and information visualization techniques.

- **Project: Visual Text Analytics** (WS 14/15). Interfaces and visualization
  metaphors for the collaborative analysis of text on a large, high-resolution
  multi-touch display, including a text reuse analysis of all of Wikipedia.

- **Project: Interactive learning with HistoGlobe** (WS 14/15). An interactive
  visualization prototype that helps high school teachers present historical
  developments, developed with the Lobdeburgschule in Jena.

- **Project: Information Visualization in Virtual Reality** (SS 14). Bringing
  scatterplots, parallel coordinates and graph drawings into immersive virtual
  reality, making use of the third dimension.

- **Project: Touch the Time** (SS 13). Time-series visualizations for displays
  with more than eight million pixels, with new touch-based interactions.

- **Project: Visualizing plagiarism II** (WS 12/13). A web-based visual
  analysis tool to verify, assess and present alleged cases of plagiarism.

- **Seminar: Hot Topics in Information Visualization** (WS 12/13). Reviewing
  and presenting current publications from InfoVis, VAST and EuroVis.
