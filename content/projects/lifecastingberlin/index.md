Website for Life Casting Berlin, the artist-led studio where sculptor Brooke
Meenan casts torsos, hands and couples and turns each cast into a sculpture
in plaster, pigmented resin, gold leaf or bronze.

The relaunch started with a written concept and a clickable prototype,
refined together with the artist over several rounds. The live site is a
custom WordPress block theme in German and English, with pages for
commissions, workshops and team events, a casting guide, and "Book a call"
as the first step towards a commission.

A companion plugin adds structured data (JSON-LD) for the studio, the artist,
every page and every work, publishes a Markdown version of each page and an
`llms.txt`, so that search engines and AI assistants understand and cite the
studio correctly.
