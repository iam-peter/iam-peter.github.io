Website for *Dreck in die Wunde*, a series of living sculptures by Kimberly
Meenan, cast from dirt collected on the streets of Berlin mixed with clay,
hair, hemp and plant seeds. As the seeds grow, the sculptures change over
time. A map links the artworks in Körnerpark and around Neukölln and
documents how they change. The site is available in German and English.
